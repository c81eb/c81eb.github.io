What is Artisan Coffee Roasting?

Artisan coffee roasting is the craft of carefully applying heat to green coffee beans to transform their chemical and physical properties.

Unlike commercial roasting, which focuses on volume, artisan roasting focuses on extracting the unique flavor notes of beans from specific regions.

  • Highlights single-origin beans.
  • Focuses on light to medium roasts to preserve acidity.
  • Emphasizes the "crack" stages of the bean.

When to Roast & Brew

Timing is everything in the world of coffee. Roasting is just the first step.

After roasting, coffee beans need to "degas" (release carbon dioxide) for roughly 48 to 72 hours before they are optimal for brewing.

ActivityIdeal Timeframe
RoastingWeekends (takes about 1 hour for setup/cleanup)
Degassing2-4 days post-roast
Peak BrewingDays 5 through 14 post-roast

Best Sourcing Locations

The origin of the green coffee bean dictates the final flavor profile in your cup.

I primarily source my green beans from small importers who practice direct trade with farmers.

  1. Ethiopia: Known for floral, tea-like, and berry notes.
  2. Colombia: Balanced, chocolatey, and nutty.
  3. Costa Rica: Clean, bright acidity, sweet honey notes.

How to Roast at Home

You don't need a massive commercial roaster to get started; many hobbyists start with a simple popcorn popper!

The key is listening for the "First Crack," which sounds like popcorn popping. This indicates the bean is expanding and the light roast stage has been reached.

Here is my standard procedure:

  1. Preheat the roasting drum to 400°F.
  2. Drop the green beans in and monitor the temperature curve.
  3. Drop the beans into a cooling tray immediately after the first crack finishes.

Why I Love This Hobby

Roasting coffee is the perfect intersection of science and culinary art.

It forces you to be present. You have to rely on your sight, smell, and hearing to know exactly when a roast is finished.

Plus, sharing a cup of coffee you roasted yourself with a friend is an incredibly rewarding experience.
