Introduction

The GeckoGrip Wall Mount holds Clever Gecko cameras and sensors on drywall, brick, or wood. Its textured pads grip the surface, and the mounting plate keeps your device steady.

Installation takes about ten minutes and needs no special skills.

What You Need

  • GeckoGrip mounting plate and four grip pads
  • Four wall screws and anchors (included)
  • Power drill with a 6 mm bit
  • Pencil and level
  • Screwdriver

Installation Steps

Follow these steps in order.

  1. Pick a spot at least 2.5 meters off the ground with a clear view of the area.
  2. Hold the mounting plate against the wall and mark the four screw holes with a pencil.
  3. Check that the marks are level, then drill a hole at each mark.
  4. Push an anchor into each hole.
  5. Screw the plate to the wall until it sits flat.
  6. Press the grip pads onto the back of your device, then slide it onto the plate until it clicks.

Troubleshooting

If the device wobbles, tighten the screws and check that all four grip pads are seated.

If it will not click into place, remove it and make sure the plate is level and flat against the wall.

For other problems, contact Clever Gecko support.
