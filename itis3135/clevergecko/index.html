Welcome to Clever Gecko

Step up to our little kiosk and find everything that clings: removable wall hooks, gel phone grips, reusable adhesive pads, and sticky-note sets. No nails, no holes, no drama.

Clever Gecko started with one simple question: why hammer a nail into the wall when you could just stick something up and peel it off later? We only sell four kinds of things, and we are really good at all of them.
